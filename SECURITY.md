# Security

## Bug bounty

Duel's contracts are covered by a bug bounty: a valid report of a vulnerability in them is rewarded with **$100 to
$5,000**, depending on its severity. Up to $5,000 goes to the most critical: a flaw that would let someone take or lock
the funds the contracts hold, the pools' ETH and tokens, the pots, the creators' and the protocol's fees.

- **Scope.** The contracts of this repository as deployed on Robinhood Chain (see [Deployments](README.md#deployments)).
  Uniswap V4's own contracts, the website, and what the documentation's
  [security page](https://divine.sh/docs/duel/contracts/security) or [risks](https://divine.sh/docs/duel/risks) already
  describe are not covered.
- **Rules.** Show the flaw on a fork or a local chain, never on live duels. Report it privately, and give us time to act
  before sharing anything. Only the first report of a flaw is rewarded.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Join DIVINE's Telegram group, [DIVINE Screener Club](https://t.me/divinescreenerclub),
and ask to talk to **chatwithdivine**: say there only that you have a vulnerability to report, never what it is. Then,
in private, tell us what you found and, if you can, how to reproduce it. We will answer quickly and keep you informed.

Only [@chatwithdivine](https://t.me/chatwithdivine) handles reports: anyone else who writes to you in DIVINE's name is
not us.
